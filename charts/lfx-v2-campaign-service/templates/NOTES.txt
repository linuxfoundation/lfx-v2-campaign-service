WARNING: TWITTER_METRICS_ENABLED is on with more than one replica (replicaCount > 1, or
autoscaling with maxReplicas > 1). The X stats-job features' per-account limits (job budget,
write pacer) are per process; only the pod holding the per-account stats-job lease creates X
stats jobs, so a replica that does not own an account's lease answers that account's X audience
reads 503 and skips its X monitor report submissions (serving the saved report). Behind a load
balancer most X audience reads will therefore 503. This is a warning, not a refusal (#381): run
one replica while the flag is on.
