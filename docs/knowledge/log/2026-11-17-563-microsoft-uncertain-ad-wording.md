# 2026-11-17 — #563 Microsoft keeps an unconfirmed ad or keyword step uncertain in the operator step

**Fix** — Greptile finding on #623. `IncompleteAdGroups` treated a missing `AdID` as proof the ad was not created, so a group whose ad group Microsoft confirmed but whose ad request timed out or returned an unreadable success left the "verify UNCONFIRMED first" wording and was told to "add the missing ad", which could make a duplicate. `AdGroupResult` now records `StepUnconfirmed` (set by `createAdGroups` when the group's error `IsOutcomeUnconfirmed` and an ad group id was recorded). `IncompleteAdGroups` names only DEFINITE ad/keyword failures; the new `UncertainAdGroups` names the unconfirmed ones ("its ad may or may not exist" / "its keywords may or may not all exist"), and `applyMicrosoftAdGroupExtras` writes them in their own verify-first step: do not create them again, verify in Microsoft Advertising before adding anything. Tests: `TestCreateAdGroups_UnconfirmedAdStepIsUncertain` and `TestMicrosoftAdGroupExtras_UnconfirmedAdStepStaysUncertain`, both failing without the fix.
