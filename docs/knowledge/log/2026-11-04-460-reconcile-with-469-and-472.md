# 2026-11-04 — #460 Demand Gen video ads and ad groups reconciled with #469, #472, #478 and #479

**Docs** — #481 (#460) was rebased onto main, which now carries #469 (#459), #472 (#462),
#478 (#473) and #479 (#476). The merge kept both sides; four things it alone left wrong or
unstated are fixed here.

- `AdGroupResult` holds both PRs' fields: #478's `NegativeKeywordCriteriaIDs` and
  `DemographicCriteriaIDs` (Search and Display) and #460's `GeoCriterionIDs`,
  `TargetingCriterionIDs` and `CreativeAssetIDs` (Demand Gen groups).
- #478's parity test expected the Search `adGroups` list on Demand Gen to be refused as "a
  SEARCH capability". #460 gives Demand Gen its own sentence, which names
  `DemandGenAdGroups`; the test now expects that sentence. The refusal still lands before
  the budget.
- #479 tags every final URL with the channel's `utm_medium`. The Demand Gen video ad's payload
  test now expects `utm_medium=display`, and the two-group test checks both ads' URLs and
  the campaign's `utmcampaign` custom parameter, which every group inherits.
- #469 renamed its exact-match step helper to `hasExactStep`, because #478's `containsStep`
  matches a substring. The video ad test meant the exact line, so it now calls
  `hasExactStep`.

#472's fences need nothing: `demandGenAdGroups` is refused off Demand Gen, Display's
audience lists and placements are refused off Display, and the two do not share an input.
