# 2026-11-14 — Meta video ads from stored MP4 assets (#550)

**Update** — `metaConfig.variants[].videoAssetId` turns a variant into a Meta video ad, behind
`META_VIDEO_ENABLED` (default off). Synchronous 400 for the gate, a non-uuid id and an image
alongside, re-checked in `Dispatch` and the client. After the ad set exists, each distinct MP4 is
uploaded once to `/act_<id>/advideos` (never retried), all videos share one 60 s readiness wait, the
preferred auto-generated thumbnail is used, and the creative carries `video_data`. A video failure
fails only its variants (`created_degraded`, nothing re-uploaded); minted ids are kept in
`CampaignResult.VideoIDs`. See [Meta Video Ads](../architecture/meta-video-ads.md).
