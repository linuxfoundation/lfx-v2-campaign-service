# 2026-11-17 — #563 Microsoft toggle reports a partial outcome once any ad group changed

**Fix** — A #613 review finding. `MicrosoftDispatcher.ToggleStatus` activates every further ad group before the first group and the campaign gate. A failure after an earlier further group had activated returned a plain error. That failure could be the next group's keyword read, its "every keyword removed" refusal, a definite rejection of its first status write, or a definite rejection of the first group's cascade. The service then said the campaign was not modified and released the write lock without the cooldown it holds for uncertain changes. An already-active campaign reaches this loop too, so the activated group may already be serving. The loop now records the further groups Microsoft confirmed Active. Once any has, every later failure is returned as `unconfirmedToggleError`, the mechanism the other toggles already use. The cause is formatted with `%v`, not `%w`, so a carried `ErrCampaignNotProvisioned` cannot reach the service's 409 arm, which sits before its unconfirmed arm. While nothing has changed, every failure stays as definite as before. Covered by `TestMicrosoftAdGroups_ToggleActivateLaterGroupFailureAfterAnActivatedGroupIsPartial` and `TestMicrosoftAdGroups_ToggleActivateFirstFurtherGroupFailureStaysDefinite`.
