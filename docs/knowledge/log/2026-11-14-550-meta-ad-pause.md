# 2026-11-14 — #550 Meta per-ad pause/resume, kept across a campaign resume (gap 14)

**Creation** — Gap 14 of the Meta gap review (#550). An operator could not pause one ad, and a
campaign resume re-activated every ad under the recorded ad set, so a later per-ad pause would not
have survived it. The owner decided (2026-11-14) that the resume cascade follows #309's ad-set rule
for ads: only what the service paused is re-activated, and a deliberate per-ad pause is recorded.

The cascade fix: the per-ad record is `UserPausedAdIDs` in the campaign row's Result blob. On
ACTIVATE, `ToggleStatus` reads each held ad first (an ad Meta reports ACTIVE refuses the resume with
409, an unreadable one is a 503, nothing written) and `ActivateCampaignHolding` skips each held ad's
POST; the held ad set rule composes with it. PAUSE still cascades to every ad.

The lever: `POST …/meta-ad-sets/{ad_set_id}/ads/{ad_id}/status` (`toggle-meta-ad-status`) mirrors
`toggle-meta-ad-set-status` end to end: Goa method and result type, If-Match and the campaign claim,
provenance, a pre-write ad read proving ad set, campaign and account, ACTIVATE only under the
recorded ad set, ALREADY_IN_STATE without a write, one unretried write classified the same way, and
the record's set/withdraw/clear rules through the same reconciliation, now shared via
`metaPauseRecord`. Gateway: it nests under `/briefs/**`, so the HTTPRoute match and the
`campaign_manager` rule cover it like its siblings; `parity_test.go` pins the path and
`TestMetaAdIDPattern_MatchesPlatformValidator` pins both id patterns. `list-meta-ads` reports
`deliberately_paused`.

Behind `META_AD_PAUSE_ENABLED` (default off): off, the endpoint is a 400. The cascade honours an
existing record whatever the flag says. Not live-verified: the ad node's `adset_id` field on the
pre-write read and the per-ad write's answer shape.

Tests: `meta_ad_status_test.go` in dispatch (held ad skipped and read first, the two holds
composing, an ACTIVE or unreadable held ad refusing with no write, PAUSE unchanged, the lever's
write, refusals and ownership, `deliberately_paused`, the client cascade), `meta_ad_status_test.go`
in service (flag, record set/kept/withdrawn/cleared, untracked ads, validation and the error map),
`meta_ad_pause_test.go` in model, the gateway parity row and the id-pattern drift test. Twenty-eight
of thirty hand mutations fail a test; the two survivors are equivalent (PAUSE never passes a held
set; the op constant's value is read only through its own name).
