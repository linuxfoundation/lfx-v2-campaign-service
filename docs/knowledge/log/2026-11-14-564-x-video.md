# 2026-11-14 — #564 X video creative

**Update** — `twitterConfig.videoAssetId` (gap 7 of the X Ads gap review, #564) uploads a stored
MP4 creative asset as `amplify_video` and attaches it to the authored tweet(s), or uses it as the
website card's media.

- `media.go` is parameterised by `mediaKind`; the video takes the image path's chunked upload
  (initialize / append / finalize / STATUS, at most 6 x 5 s of waiting) and Media Library add,
  before the campaign POST, so every failure creates nothing and a library entry is reused rather
  than re-uploaded.
- A create with a video may name at most 30 targeting criteria; never with `imageAssetId`;
  requires an authored tweet.
- New default-off flag `TWITTER_VIDEO_ENABLED`; synchronous 400s re-checked in `Dispatch`.
- Builds on the MP4 asset store taken unchanged from #586 (migration `000057`).

Not verified live: X's processing time for a 20-30 MiB MP4 must be measured against the 30 s bound
before enabling.
