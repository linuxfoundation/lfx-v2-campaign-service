# 2026-11-17 — #563 Microsoft activate reads share one budget; created-but-failed groups are named

**Fix** — Two Cursor Bugbot findings on #623. First, ACTIVATE on a multi-ad-group campaign read each further group's live keywords under its own 10s `microsoftActivateKeywordReadBudget`, interleaved with that group's status writes, all from the toggle's 45s deadline: five reads could outlast it after some groups were already set Active and before the campaign gate opened. `ToggleStatus` now reads every group's keywords (the first and each further one) BEFORE ANY status write, under ONE shared context from `microsoftActivateReadContext`: the budget is the smaller of `microsoftActivateKeywordReadBudget` and half the caller's remaining deadline, so the writes keep at least the other half. A failed or timed-out read, or a group with no live keyword, now refuses DEFINITELY with nothing activated; the partial-toggle semantics (`unconfirmedToggleError` once any group changed) still cover the writes. Second, `FailedAdGroups` treated every failed entry as never created, and `applyMicrosoftAdGroupExtras` told the operator to create it, even when Microsoft had confirmed the ad group before its ad or keyword step failed. `FailedAdGroups` now names only entries with no confirmed ad group of their own; the new `IncompleteAdGroups` names the rest with the ad group id and the step left ("its ad (and so its keywords) was not created" or "its keywords were not all created"), and the dispatcher writes a separate "created in Microsoft but NOT completed ... Do not create them again" step. Tests: `TestMicrosoftActivate_EveryKeywordReadPrecedesEveryWrite`, `TestMicrosoftActivate_ReadsShareOneBudget`, `TestMicrosoftActivate_ReadsLeaveHalfTheDeadlineForTheWrites`, `TestMicrosoftActivate_LaterGroupReadFailureIsDefinite`, `TestMicrosoftAdGroupExtras_FailedGroupWording`, `TestMicrosoftAdGroupExtras_NoIncompleteStepWithoutAConfirmedGroup` and `TestFailedAndIncompleteAdGroups`; all but the no-incomplete guard fail without the fix. The two read-failure cases of `TestMicrosoftAdGroups_ToggleActivateLaterGroupFailureAfterAnActivatedGroupIsPartial` moved to the definite test, since a read can no longer follow a write.
